## Plan

Two clean-ups that [phase 4](2026-11-06-f-phase4.md) made necessary before any
further number on the Kurrent family.

## Done

- **Collapse detector** ([A2](../amendments.md#a2)): a repetition loop now
  needs the most frequent trigram at least six times *and* at least twice as
  often as in the reference, plus two. The old test stays available so earlier
  decisions remain reproducible; those decisions stand as recorded.
- **`blb-eval-hand`**, the Kurrent family's held-out set from now on, chosen by
  **data properties only** — no rule uses a model's output, because choosing
  test lines by how a model reads them would bias every number measured on
  them. Excluded: the printed book (2,825 lines), a typescript (372), a printed
  form, music notation, a folded letter's outside, vertical crops, 1–3-character
  references, boxes holding two lines. Kept: **2,272 lines, 29 objects**,
  audited clean against every practice list.
