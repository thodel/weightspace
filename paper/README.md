# paper/

Draft of the article for the Journal of Data Mining & Digital Humanities. Read `STATUS.md` first.

- `manuscript.md`: the article (pandoc Markdown, citations as `[@key]`).
- `references.bib`: exported from the Zotero collection *WSP – analysis & explainability of text-recognition
  networks* and the framing references of the introduction. Regenerate from Zotero; do not edit by hand
  except to correct an export artefact.
- `build.sh`: builds `build/manuscript.docx` and `build/manuscript.tex`; needs pandoc 2.11 or newer.
- `STATUS.md`: narrative-to-section map, open items, decisions, submission steps.
- Figures are generated by `wsp.viz` into `protocol/docs/figures/`; the manuscript links to them.
