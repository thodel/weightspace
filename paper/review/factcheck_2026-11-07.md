# Fact-check of paper/manuscript.md (draft of 7 Nov 2026)

Method: every sentence with a number, date, name, count, quotation, causal statement or "we did / did not" was compared with the sources named in the brief. Tables 1 to 8 were checked cell by cell; Table 4 and the held-out tables were recomputed from `docs/baselines/*.json`, Table 3 and the conv-versus-LSTM claim from `docs/readings/diff_*.json`, Sections 7.3, 7.4 and 8.1 from `runs/*/log.jsonl`, `preregistration.md`, `notes.md` and `summary.json`. Section 3.1 was checked against the saved blog text (`jdmdh/blog.txt`), Section 5 against `docs/RELATED_WORK.md`, `docs/SEARCH_LOG.md` and a sample of `docs/related_work/notes/*.md`.

Line numbers: "ms L" = manuscript.md line. Everything not listed in the problem table was found SUPPORTED (about 150 claims; not itemised). All of Tables 1, 2, 4 (every cell), 5, 6, 7 and 8 match their sources except the points listed. Tables are numbered 1 to 8 in order of appearance and every "Table N" in the text points to the right table.

Totals of problems: WRONG 12, OVERSTATED 18, UNSUPPORTED 5.
