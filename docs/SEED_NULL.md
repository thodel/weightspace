# Seed-variance null for the weight readings

Written 07.11.2026 on asteraix. Question (reviewer): how much of the "trace of fine-tuning" of `paper/manuscript.md` Section 6.3 is present
between independent runs that differ only by seed? Code: `scripts/seed_null_prepare.py`, `seed_null_train.sh`, `seed_null_all.sh`, `seed_null_curves.py`,
`seed_null_compare.py` (uses `wsp.zoo.pair_reading`, the file of `claude/zoo-survey-76f514`, copied unchanged), `seed_null_eval.py`, `seed_null_tables.py`.
Data under `docs/seed-null/`: `split.json`, `train.jsonl`, `val.jsonl`, `audit.json`, `runs.json` (per-epoch validation accuracy), `readings.json`/`readings.txt`,
`heldout.json` (+ `heldout.hyp.json`, every hypothesis), `tables.md` (the tables below, regenerated by `seed_null_tables.py`). New weights stay in
`~/atr-cache/wsp/seed-null/seed*/` (never exported, registered, uploaded or served).

## 1. Method

- **Parent:** `cursive_inzigkofen` (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.18207767; sha256 `4ab19e7b…`, identical to the trainer's base file `cursive_johannes_jaeck.mlmodel`).
- **Data:** `dh-unibe/image-text_bern-disputation-1528` revision `28a16d55` (22.10.2026), project `A_V_1443_Hertwig_train`, 50 pages, parquet sha256 `566f5bd1…`
  (not the 25.10 export, whose images are mismatched, `docs/HERTWIG_REVISIONS.md`). Split by page, seed 42: 45 training pages (1,771 lines), 5 validation pages (213 lines),
  identical for every seed. The trainer's own split of `hertwig-v2` (1,786 / 198 lines, partition 0.9, seed 42) could not be reproduced: its arrow artefact had been purged, so the
  split is mine and the pages differ a little. **Audit** (`python -m wsp.audit overlap`, `audit.json`): both lists clean, none of the Hertwig test pages (those of `data/heldout/bern-eval.jsonl`,
  a different project) is in training or validation. Lines were compiled with `ketos compile -f page` (kraken's polygon extractor).
- **Hyperparameters** (the trainer's record of `hertwig-v2`, `job.json`): batch 16, AdamW (kraken default), cosine schedule with kraken's default `--cos-max 10` (the trainer did not set it), learning rate 5e-4,
  augmentation on, NFD, whitespace normalised, `--resize union`, `--weights-format coreml`, kraken 7.0.2, the same base. Differences, all stated:
  (i) **fixed 91 epochs** (`--quit fixed`, no early stopping), v2 was planned for 150 and stopped by early stopping at 91; (ii) my validation split; (iii) the weights kept are the checkpoint with the best validation accuracy (as the trainer kept `best_0.7678` of v2, epoch 81),
  here epochs 84–89 (T1); (iv) seeds 1–5 (`ketos --seed`, which seeds torch and numpy: initial values of the rows new to the codec, batch order, augmentation, dropout); the data and the split do not change.
  Validation accuracy per epoch of every run is in `runs.json`; the values reach 0.711–0.716 (not comparable with v2's 0.768: other pages).
- **Route.** `ketos train` from this repo's venv (kraken 7.0.2, torch of the trainer), **not** the trainer's job mechanism: that route writes into the trainer's `trained/` tree and registers a model, which this work must not do.
  The first attempt was CPU, 12 threads: about 12 minutes per epoch with the box at load 70 from other jobs (about 18 h for 91 epochs), so the run was stopped. On the starting session's approval the five runs went to **GPU card 0** (`CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0`; card 1 never touched), one at a time,
  each started only after `nvidia-smi` showed at least 12 GB free there (free before the start 18–25 GB, `runs.json`), and the card was free of my process between runs. Card 0 peaks in T1 are for the whole card (neighbours included); the trainer recorded about 4 GB own memory for the v2 run. No other process was touched.
- **Readings** (`wsp.zoo.pair_reading`, `wsp.stats` definitions of `docs/READINGS.md`): relative L2 per block, output rows aligned by character, blank bias raw and relative to the mean of the other biases, rare-row inflation with the four criteria (a)–(d) of `docs/ZOO_SURVEY.md` T5 (proxy: characters outside basic Latin, digits, space, ASCII punctuation; frequent = `e n i a r t s o u d`), and which stack moves (pooled conv against pooled LSTM, first conv block).
  Pairs: all 10 pairs of the five runs, each run against the parent, against `hertwig-v2`, against `hertwig-v1`, and `v1` vs `v2`.
- **Held-out** (`data/heldout/bern-eval.jsonl`, 545 lines: 287 Hertwig, 258 Schlussredaktion), CPU, each model scored once, no selection on this set; paired per-line bootstrap (2,000 resamples, seed 20261106 of `wsp.metrics`) against `hertwig-v2`.

## 3. What the tables say

1. **The size of the change.** Two runs that differ only by seed are 0.12–0.15 apart in the output rows, 0.24 in the pooled conv stack, 0.28 in the pooled LSTM stack. The same recipe moves a run from its parent by 0.41 (rows), 0.27 and 0.31 (stacks); so **seed noise is about a third of the movement in the rows and about 90 % of it in the pooled stacks**. The paper's sibling pair (0.16 / 0.10–0.17) is the same size as seed noise; it was not a lucky pair.
   Seed noise is also about half of the weak fine-tune `parent → v1` (0.26 rows; its stacks, 0.12 and 0.13, are *smaller* than the noise between seeds: v1 was a short, low-rate run, and its distance to a seed run, 0.19, is larger than the distance between two seeds, 0.13). By block (`readings.json`): the first conv block `C_0` differs 0.09–0.14 between seeds and moves 0.14–0.15 from the parent; `L_12` differs 0.153–0.157 between seeds and moves 0.15 from the parent. For these two blocks the size of the change is not distinguishable from seed noise; for `L_14`, `L_16` (0.14, 0.13 noise against 0.17, 0.18) and the output rows it is.
2. **Blank bias.** Between seeds the raw change is −0.004 to +0.008 and relative to the other biases −0.024 to +0.013; parent → run is +0.06 raw (0.057–0.065) and **+0.53–0.55 relative**, in every run, reproduced to the second digit by `v2` (+0.52). The rise is a systematic effect of fine-tuning on this data, not a seed effect. (`v1 → v2` shows +0.20 relative, but that is entirely v1's: v1 against each seed run shows the same +0.2, seed against seed 0.)
3. **Rare rows.** Between seeds the special rows do not grow (median norm ratio 0.99–1.00, parent → run 1.23), their bias does not fall (median Δ −0.02 to +0.03 against −0.73 to −0.77) and they move *less* than the frequent letters (0.10–0.13 against 0.14–0.15; criterion (c) holds in 0 of 10 seed pairs and 5 of 5 parent → run pairs). Criteria (a) and (b) hold in 3 and 2 of 10 seed pairs (chance level), 5 of 5 for parent → run. All four hold in 0 of 10 seed pairs, 0 of 5 for v2 → run and v1 → run, and in all 5 parent → run pairs, `parent → v1` and `parent → v2`. Criterion (d) (the top decile contains at least the special share) holds in 10 of 10 seed pairs: it does not discriminate, because the rows that differ most between seeds are the characters that are **new to the codec** (`" „ ♀ ̅ ♂`, relative L2 0.48–0.72), whose rows start from a random initial value that depends on the seed, and these are special characters.
4. **Which stack moves.** The first conv block moves least of the four conv blocks in 10 of 10 seed pairs, in 5 of 5 runs against the parent and in `v1 → v2`; the pooled conv is below the pooled LSTM in all 10 seed pairs and all 5 parent → run pairs. The depth pattern of Section 6.3 therefore appears equally in noise (C_0 < C_3 ≤ C_6 ≤ C_9) and is no evidence of a specific effect of fine-tuning. The recurrent blocks go the other way: between seeds they are `L_12` > `L_14` > `L_16` (0.155, 0.14, 0.13), from the parent `L_12` < `L_14` < `L_16` (0.15, 0.17, 0.18), so the increase with depth in the recurrent stack is a fine-tuning effect.
5. **Held-out CER.** Five seeds on `bern-eval`: CER 27.20–27.65 % over all lines (mean 27.45, sd 0.18 points), 23.29–23.97 % on the Hertwig lines (sd 0.28), `hertwig-v2` 27.49 / 23.64. Collapsed lines 20–26 of 545 (v2 23), 10–14 of 287 on Hertwig (v2 11). Every seed's paired interval against v2 contains 0, widths about ±0.5 points; of the 10 seed pairs two have a Hertwig interval that excludes 0 (seed 3 vs seed 2 +0.67 [+0.05, +1.29], seed 5 vs seed 3 −0.68 [−1.28, −0.01]), none over all lines. The intervals are over lines only; the spread over seeds is of the same order (range 0.45 points all, 0.68 Hertwig). **Differences below about one point of CER between two runs of this recipe are not evidence of anything**, and the 2.9 points between v1 and v2 (interval +2.3 to +3.5) are.

## 4. For Section 6.3 of the manuscript

*Two independent runs of the same recipe, from the same parent, on the same data and differing only by seed (five runs, 91 epochs each, `docs/SEED_NULL.md`), differ by 0.12 to 0.15 in the output rows and 0.10 to 0.21 in the individual convolutional blocks, against 0.41 and 0.14 to 0.24 between the parent and the same runs. The sibling pair of the earlier version (`hertwig-v1`, `hertwig-v2`, 0.16) therefore differs by about what seeds differ by, and the size of a change in weights, taken alone, does not separate a fine-tuning effect from seed noise: for the first convolutional block (0.09 to 0.14 between seeds, 0.14 to 0.15 from the parent) and the first recurrent block (0.153 to 0.157, 0.15) it does not separate them at all. Four observations survive the null. The blank bias relative to the other biases rises by 0.53 to 0.55 in every run and does not change between seeds (−0.02 to +0.01); the raw rise of 0.06 likewise (seeds: −0.004 to +0.008). The rows of rare characters grow and lose bias only in parent-to-child pairs (median norm ratio 1.23, median bias change −0.73 to −0.77, against 1.00 and −0.02 to +0.03 between seeds), where they also move more than the frequent letters; between seeds they move less. The rows that differ most between seeds are those of characters new to the codec, whose initial values are random. The recurrent stack moves more with depth only from the parent. Two statements do not survive: that the first convolutional block "hardly moves" and that the movement grows with depth in the convolutional blocks are properties of seed noise as well and are not evidence of fine-tuning. The fine-tuned model's held-out CER varies by a standard deviation of 0.2 points over seeds (0.3 on the Hertwig pages), so that differences of less than one point between two runs cannot be interpreted. Limits: one parent, one data set, one recipe; the null is of the recipe of `hertwig-v2` with my own page split and a fixed 91 epochs, not a reproduction of its exact run, and the criteria (a) to (d) use a proxy for rarity (no corpus frequencies).*

## 5. What went wrong or was not done

- The trainer's own `hertwig-v2` split and arrows were not recoverable (artefact purged): the null uses a different 5-page validation split, which is fixed across seeds; v2 is therefore a comparison, not a member of the null.
- The CPU route, the one named as the fallback, was too slow under the box's load and was abandoned after one epoch; GPU card 0 was used on the starting session's approval.
- GPU memory of the run alone was not sampled (card totals only).
- One parent, one data set; the null is not a statement about other recipes. Selection of the checkpoint by validation accuracy is part of the recipe and its epoch is recorded (T1).
