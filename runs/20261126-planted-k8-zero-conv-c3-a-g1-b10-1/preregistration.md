## Diagnosis from Pack Readings
1. **Primary planted defect (`C_3` zeroed):** In `describe.md`, `C_3.co.weight` ([32, 32, 3, 13]) and `C_3.co.bias` ([32]) both have L2 = 0.00, rms = 0.0000, abs max = 0.000. In `diff_parent_to_subject.md`, `C_3.co.weight` and `C_3.co.bias` have `rel L2 = 1.0000` and `cosine = nan` with 100% of elements changed. Because the VGSL architecture is a sequential conv stack (`C_0 -> Mp -> C_3 -> Mp -> C_6 -> Mp -> C_9`) with ReLU activations and no residual connections, zeroing `C_3` completely kills all image signal reaching `C_6` and subsequent layers, causing 100% blank emissions on `c0_practice.md` (CER 1.0000, 300/300 collapsed lines).
2. **Potential secondary anomaly (`L_16.layer.weight_ih_l0` abs max):** All other layers (`C_0`, `C_6`, `C_9`, `L_12`, `L_14`, `L_16`, `O_18`) show normal fine-tuning distances from the parent (`rel L2` 0.12–0.33, cosines 0.945–0.999). Notably, `L_16.layer.weight_ih_l0` has `abs max = 4.702` (vs ~1.8–2.8 in other LSTM input-hidden matrices), though its `rel L2` from parent is a normal `0.2964` (`cosine = 0.95626`). We will test whether this is a secondary defect or normal parent/fine-tuning structure after repairing `C_3`.

## Hypotheses

- **H1 (`transplant` on `C_3.co.weight` and `C_3.co.bias` from parent `kraken-german-xix-v1`):** Transplanting the parent's `C_3.co.weight` and `C_3.co.bias` into the zeroed `C_3` block will restore signal propagation through the convolutional backbone; **predicted CER change:** drops from `1.0000` to `< 0.1500` (a reduction of `0.85` to `0.96` CER points, uncollapsing almost all 300 lines). *If it fails:* If the model remains collapsed or CER stays high (> 0.50), either another layer is also broken or `C_6` cannot process the parent's `C_3` feature scale directly without gain adjustment.
- **H2 (`scale` on `C_3.co.weight` and `C_3.co.bias` to match child/parent norm ratio of neighboring conv layers):** Fine-tuning changed `C_0` (`rel L2` 0.12) and `C_6` (`rel L2` 0.26); scaling the transplanted `C_3` weights by the geometric mean of the child-to-parent L2 norm ratios of `C_0.co.weight` and `C_6.co.weight` (or a small gain adjustment if that ratio is ~1.0) may better align activation magnitudes entering `C_6`; **predicted CER change:** small decrease of `-0.001` to `-0.005` CER points relative to H1. *If it fails:* The raw parent `C_3` norm is already well-matched to `C_6` (due to ReLU positive homogeneity and downstream bias/LSTM saturation), and we revert to the exact parent `C_3` transplant.
- **H3 (`set_rows` / targeted replacement of outlier entries in `L_16.layer.weight_ih_l0` from parent if `abs max = 4.702` differs from parent):** If the 4.702 outlier in `L_16.layer.weight_ih_l0` is not present in the parent (i.e. a sparse spike was planted or emerged in `L_16.layer.weight_ih_l0`), replacing rows containing large deviations (`|child - parent| > 2.0`) with the parent's rows will remove the spike; **predicted CER change:** `-0.001` to `-0.010` CER points if it was a planted spike, or `~0.000` if benign. *If it fails:* The 4.702 entry is a normal feature of the parent/fine-tuned model and should be left untouched.
- **H4 (`interpolate` on `C_6.co.weight` and `C_6.co.bias` slightly toward parent, `alpha=0.10`):** Since `C_3` is now the exact parent `C_3` rather than the child's fine-tuned `C_3`, `C_6` (which takes `C_3`'s output directly) might have co-adapted to the missing child `C_3` perturbation; blending `C_6` by `alpha=0.10` toward the parent may reduce feature mismatch at the `C_3 -> C_6` interface; **predicted CER change:** `-0.001` to `-0.005` CER points. *If it fails:* The child's `C_6` fine-tuning on the target Kurrent hand outweighs any minor interface mismatch with parent `C_3`, so we keep `C_6` at 100% child weights.
